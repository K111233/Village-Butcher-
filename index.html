What’s in

On the counter

It changes with the day and the season. If you’re after something in particular, like a tomahawk for the weekend, give the shop a ring or send a message first.

  • Sausages

    Homemade, plus Lashford’s

    A new homemade flavour every week, such as pork and sweet chilli, cheese and jalapeño, or plum and ginger, alongside plain, Cumberland and tomato. This week’s flavour goes up on Facebook. Lashford’s are made by a Birmingham family firm that has been making sausages since 1889.

  • Pies, Scotch eggs & black pudding

    Pork pies, meat pies and more

    Pork pies (the pork and apple is a favourite), steak, chicken and mushroom, and lamb pies, plus Scotch eggs and black pudding.

  • Beef & steaks

    Rib eye, sirloin, fillet and tomahawk

    Plus braising cuts like beef short ribs, ideal for slow cooking.

  • Lamb

    Southdown lamb, cut how you like it

    Cut to your liking at the counter.

  • Pork

    Loin chops and more

  • Chicken

    Fresh and ready-marinated

After something in particular? Message the shop on Facebook or pop in to ask. Weekly specials go up on Facebook too.

The shop

New sign, same blue tiles

Clayton Fern served Alvechurch for 50 years before retiring from this shop in June 2024, and the village was left without a butcher.

That August, Steven Fowler opened the doors again as Village Butchers. He started learning butchery at 16 and had 28 years in the trade when he took on the shop. The traditional cuts and joints are still on the counter, alongside a new homemade sausage every week.

The shop sometimes closes early or for the day, for example on very hot days, and bank holiday hours can differ. Changes go up on the shop’s Facebook page (opens in a new tab), or give the shop a ring.

  • Parking. Tanyard Lane car park is behind The Red Lion, about two minutes’ walk. There’s more parking by The Square.
  • By bus. The 182 and 183 (Redditch to Bromsgrove) stop on Red Lion Street, a minute or two from the door.
  • By train. Alvechurch station, on the Cross-City line, is about a 14-minute walk.
